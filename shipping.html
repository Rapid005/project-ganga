Shipping & returns

Shipping

  • Where: all serviceable PIN codes in India.
  • Dispatch: within 1–2 working days of your order.
  • Delivery: usually 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Charges: free on orders over ₹999; a flat ₹79 below that.
  • Tracking: we send a tracking link by SMS and email once your order ships.

Damaged or leaking bottles

We pack every bottle in leak-proof, cushioned packaging, but accidents can happen in transit. If anything arrives damaged or leaking, send a photo of the package and the item to us within 48 hours of delivery and we’ll send a free replacement.

Returns

Because Gangajal is a sacred item, we can’t accept returns of opened bottles. Unopened items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Once we receive the item, we refund the item price to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. Shipping charges are not refundable.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order at any time before it ships for a full refund. Contact us with your order number.
