How it works

From the Ganga to your home

  1. Collected at dawn

    Our team collects water at each ghat in the early morning, before the crowds arrive.

  2. Filtered & sealed

    The water is filtered to remove sediment and sealed on the spot with a tamper-evident cap.

  3. Packed with care

    Every bottle is labelled with its source and packed in leak-proof, cushioned boxes.

  4. Delivered to you

    Your order is shipped within 1–2 days and reaches most of India in 3–7 days.
