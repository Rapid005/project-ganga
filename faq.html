About our Gangajal

Is this real Gangajal?

Yes. Every bottle is collected directly from the Ganga at the place printed on its label, and sealed there with a tamper-evident cap.

Can I drink it?

No. Our Gangajal is meant for puja and ritual use only. It is river water that has been filtered for sediment but is not treated to drinking-water standards, so please do not drink it or use it for cooking.

How is the water collected?

Our team collects water at each ghat early in the morning, filters it to remove sediment and seals it on the spot. Each batch is labelled with its source.

How long does it keep?

Kept sealed and away from direct sunlight, Gangajal keeps for a long time — many families keep it for years. Once opened, store it in a clean, closed container.

Which source should I choose?

Many families choose the place connected to their own traditions — Haridwar and Kashi are the most popular. If you’re unsure, or buying a gift, our Pancha Tirtha set includes all five.
