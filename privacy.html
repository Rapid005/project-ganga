Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information The Holy Drop collects when you use this website, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, mobile number and delivery address, so we can deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form.
  • Your cart: the items in your cart are saved in your own browser so they’re still there when you come back. They are not sent to us until you place an order.

How we use it

We use your information only to process and deliver your orders, provide customer support and meet our legal obligations, such as keeping invoices. We don’t sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share only what’s needed with our payment provider (to take payment) and courier partners (to deliver your order).

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us. You can clear your saved cart by clearing your browser’s site data.
